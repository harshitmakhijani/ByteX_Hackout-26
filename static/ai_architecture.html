ALGAEVERSE: Model 1 & Model 2

A simple, step-by-step visual map showing how satellite imagery and IoT probes are transformed into verified carbon credits.

Model 1: Satellite Vision & Bloom Detector
Deep Learning (ResNet-18) + Chlorophyll Color Analysis
Input (Satellite Image)
Satellite Picture RGB / Sentinel-2
Aerial photo of the lake (either Sentinel-2 satellite capture or direct photo upload).
Total Catchment Area Hectares (ha)
Total lake boundary size (e.g., 50.0 hectares) used to scale pixel calculations.
➜
How Model 1 Works
🛰️
ResNet-18 Vision AI
Convolutional Neural Network
1
Color & Texture Recognition Deep ResNet-18 neural network scans the scene to identify algae severity patterns.
2
Chlorophyll Color Index (ExG) Calculates green intensity (2×Green − Red − Blue) to isolate living algae from water.
3
Water Masking Ignores shoreline and clouds so only lake surface pixels are counted.
➜
Outputs (3 Visual Metrics)
Bloom Area e.g. 30.0 ha
Exact surface area covered by green algae on the lake.
Coverage % e.g. 60.0%
Percentage of the total water body covered by algae.
Severity Level Low / Med / High
Bloom density: Low (thin film), Moderate, or High (thick green scum).
🔗 Automated Hand-off: Model 1's visual results combine with 4 live IoT water probes to feed directly into Model 2.
Next: Carbon Calculation
Model 2: Carbon Sequestration & Economics Engine
XGBoost Multi-Output Machine Learning Regressor
7 Inputs (Visual + Probes)
From Model 1 (Above)
Bloom Area + Coverage % + Severity
Size, surface coverage, and thickness of the algae colony.
From IoT Water Probes
Water Temp °C
Warm water (~27°C) speeds up growth; cold stops it.
pH Level pH
Is water balanced? (Algae loves slightly alkaline 7.5–8.5).
Dissolved Oxygen mg/L
Shows active photosynthesis vs rotting decay (< 2.0 suffocates fish).
Sunlight W/m²
Solar energy powering photosynthesis.

How Model 2 Works
⚡
XGBoost ML Regressor
300 Boosted Decision Trees
1
Trained on 15,000 Scenarios Learned how temperature, light, pH, and oxygen interact together.
2
Golden Biology Chemistry Rule 1 kg dry algae = 1.83 kg CO₂ absorbed
3
Anoxia Safety Alarm If oxygen drops below 2.0 mg/L, alerts operators to aerate water and save the fish.
➜
4 Final Outputs (Value & Alerts)
Daily Biomass Yield kg / day
Total dry weight of algae grown today (e.g. 675 kg/day).
Daily CO₂ Sequestered kg CO₂ / day
Harmful greenhouse gas removed from air today (e.g. 993 kg).
Monthly CO₂ Projection Metric Tons
Total cumulative carbon trapped in 30 days (e.g. 26.8 tons/mo).
Carbon Credit Earnings $ USD
Market value: 26.8 tons × $35 = $916.90 USD / month.
Ecosystem Health Status Real-Time
Action prompt: Leave alone, Harvest now, or Emergency aeration.
